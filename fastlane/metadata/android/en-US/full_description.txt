AppLingo lets you set a different language for each installed app on Android 13 and newer, even for apps that do not expose their own language setting.

How it works:
- Connect through Root or Shizuku (wireless debugging, no root needed).
- Search your apps by name or package, including system apps.
- Pick a language, reset an app to Follow system, or apply one language to several apps at once.
- Undo the last change with a persistent, verified history.
- Pin favorites and filter to them. Refresh the currently shown languages.
- Supports work profiles and secondary Android users.

Privacy:
- No internet permission. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Only per-app language preferences are read or written. App data is never cleared.
- Favorites and undo history stay on device and are excluded from backups.

Notes:
- Android 13+ is required.
- The target app must already contain the translation you select; AppLingo cannot add missing translations.
- Language overrides remain after uninstalling AppLingo.
